About Me

Hello, I'm Rochelle.

A final year student at Singapore Management University, pursuing a Double Degree in Business Management (Finance Analytics) and Information Systems (Business Analytics). I sit at the intersection of data, business strategy, and technology — and that's exactly where I want to build my career.

I'm passionate about Product Management because it is the one role that demands both sides of my brain. My analytics background taught me to let data guide decisions, not gut feel alone — to ask why users behave a certain way, then design solutions that are measurable and intentional. My business training taught me that a great product means nothing without a clear market, a viable model, and stakeholders who believe in the vision.

What drives me is the moment a messy problem becomes a clear product solution — when user research, business constraints, and technical feasibility click into place. I want to build products that are not just functional, but genuinely make people's lives easier.
